## Where the hell is the hash key on a Mac? And other stories.

I seemed to have accidentally become a Mac fan after being given a
MacBook Pro by my awesome <a href="http://www.ibuildings.co.uk">employers</a>. Making the transition from Linux to Mac was pretty seamless... except for the keyboard. My fingers muscle memory is going to have to be retrained quite hard to make the adjustment. Just a couple of things I want to get off my chest:

* Why does the command for closing an application (<span class='keyboard-key'>cmd</span> + <span class='keyboard-key'>q</span>) have to be right next to the command to switching applications (<span class='keyboard-key'>cmd</span> + <span class='keyboard-key'>tab</span>). I have lost track of how many times I have closed an application rather than switching to the one behind it
* Why do commands change when in a terminal? Go to the end of a line in the rest of OSX is (<span class='keyboard-key'>cmd</span> + <span class='keyboard-key'>right arrow</span>) but in the terminal it does nothing (or worse: in iTerm it switches tab!). 
* Why are there seemly two names for the same key? What the hell is 'option' key or the '^' key? No keys on my MacBook Pro say 'option'. It turns out 'option' is actually  <span class='keyboard-key'>alt</span> and the '^' key means <span class='keyboard-key'>ctrl</span>.
