I've kept meaning to look up how to change case in Netbeans since my guess at Ctrl+U and Ctrl+L didn't work. So I did: 

Change selection to Uppercase:

    Ctrl+U U

Change selection to Lowercase:

    Ctrl+U L

To invert the case of the current selection:    

    Ctrl+U S

Thanks to <a href="http://netbeans6.blogspot.com/2008/03/switch-from-uppercase-to-lowercase.html">Netbeans 6</a> for that. It is also in the manual if you're the kind of person that reads manuals
